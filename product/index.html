CPS-0033: DRep Voting Power Concentration

I co-authored CPS-0033 after governance research, stakeholder interviews, workshops and wider ecosystem discussion surfaced a recurring concern around concentrated DRep voting power.

Turning that concern into a useful problem statement required more than documenting complaints. The scope had to become specific enough to be technically meaningful without prematurely prescribing a solution.

Through a 29 commit public GitHub review cycle, I responded to editor feedback, identified gaps, narrowed the scope and substantially reframed the document around ledger rules, CIP-0119 metadata, wallet behaviour and explorer behaviour. Review covered admissibility, structure, technical references, Markdown and the boundaries of the problem itself.
